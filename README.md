# SPECTREM EXPORT

A Windows companion app for **Spectrem Director** that turns your FiveM cinematics into MP4 videos.

[Download SPECTREM EXPORT](https://github.com/Spectrematozoide/SpectremExport/releases)

## How it works

Create your cinematic in Spectrem Director and keep SPECTREM EXPORT running on the same PC as FiveM. When you start an export from Director, the app captures the visible game window and saves the video to your selected folder.

Use **EXPORT** for a direct MP4 video, or **ACTION** to record for editing in Rockstar Editor.

## Getting started

1. Download the portable ZIP from [Releases](https://github.com/Spectrematozoide/SpectremExport/releases).
2. Extract the entire ZIP and open `SpectremExport.exe`.
3. Let the app finish its automatic setup, then choose your output folder in **Settings**.
4. Open FiveM and prepare your cinematic in Spectrem Director.
5. Select **EXPORT** in Director and wait for the readiness checks to pass before starting.
6. Let the export finish, or use **Stop and Save** to end it. Wait for completion before closing the app.

## Features

- Direct MP4 export controlled from Spectrem Director.
- Automatic encoder selection, with manual selection available.
- Custom output folder.
- Automatic setup of the required video tools.
- Export status and activity logs.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- .NET Framework 4.8.
- FiveM and a compatible version of Spectrem Director.
- Internet access for the initial video-tool setup.

## Current limitations

Exports capture the game in real time, so keep the game window visible. Video quality and smoothness depend on your PC and game performance. Audio is not currently included.
